Our apps

Five apps, each doing one job well. Every app has its own privacy policy that says exactly what data it uses.

Odak: AI To Do & Planner

Productivity · Free with optional Odak Plus

Your AI coach, planner, calendar and notes — one focused app to get things done.

  • Tasks, calendar and notes in one place, with reminders that ring on time
  • LUMO, an AI coach that helps you plan the day and add tasks by voice
  • Location reminders, home-screen widgets and a Wear OS companion
Google Play Privacy policy

NotifyOS: Notification Rules

Tools · Paid app

Silence notifications by time, place or calendar. Nothing is ever lost.

  • A rule engine for "quiet, unless…" — conditions, actions and templates
  • Every captured notification goes into a searchable archive on your phone
  • No internet permission: your notifications cannot leave the device
Google Play Privacy policy

Dead Code

Game · Typing

A terminal-style typing game: type each process's word to kill it before it reaches you.

  • Waves and Sections modes with enemies that freeze, cloak, split and mirror
  • English and Turkish word lists
  • Optional online mode: one-on-one matches and a public leaderboard
Google Play Privacy policy

Ring Rider

Game · Arcade

A fast arcade game: ride through the rings, level after level.

  • Level mode plus Asteroid Survival and black-hole modes
  • Collect stars and coins, unlock skins in the coin shop
  • Daily rewards and missions
Google Play Privacy policy

Sentinel: Anti-Theft Alarm

Security · Paid app

A loud siren on the phone itself when it's moved, unplugged or taken from a pocket. Only your PIN silences it.

  • 13 modes — Desk, Charging, Pocket/Bag, Night, Travel and more — plus scheduled protection
  • Alarm after wrong unlock attempts, and an event history with PDF reports
  • No internet permission, no account, no ads — a one-time purchase
Google Play Privacy policy

How we build

A few rules every app follows.

On your device first

Your tasks, notes, notifications, alarm history and game progress are stored on your phone. Where data has to leave it, the privacy policy says so plainly.

We don't sell data

None of our apps sell your personal data. Ads, where they exist, are served by Google AdMob under Google's policies.

One job, done well

Small, focused apps instead of bloated ones — each built to solve one everyday problem properly.
